# Stillfeed

## Short description

Turn Instagram, X, and LinkedIn grayscale, including videos. Pause for a few minutes or keep a single tab in color.

## Store description

Keep the feed. Turn down the color. Stillfeed displays Instagram, X, and LinkedIn in grayscale as you browse, including photos and videos playing on the page.

Open the toolbar popup to choose which sites use grayscale. Choose Color or Grayscale for just the current tab, or select Site setting to follow your usual preference. Pause all three sites for 2, 5, or 10 minutes and Stillfeed resumes automatically. You can also resume early.

Site preferences stay in your browser. Stillfeed runs locally and collects no data.

## Dashboard details

English. Suggested Chrome category: Workflow & Planning. Suggested Firefox category: Appearance.

Single purpose: apply user-controlled grayscale to the three supported social sites.

Storage saves site preferences and the pause deadline locally and keeps tab overrides in session memory. Alarms restore grayscale when a pause ends. Host access lets Stillfeed apply the display setting to matching tabs and update them together. X includes its former twitter.com domain. The extension makes no network requests, includes no remote code, and transmits no data.

## Reviewer steps

Open a supported site after installation (reload an already-open feed). Play an inline video. Open a second tab on the same site, then choose Color in the first tab. Compare the two tabs. Choose Site setting to reset. Pause for two minutes, check both tabs, then leave them open through expiry. An unrelated site keeps its original colors.
