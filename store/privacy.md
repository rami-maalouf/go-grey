# Go Grey privacy policy

Go Grey runs on your device. The extension collects no personal data and sends no information to its developer, advertisers, or analytics services.

## What stays in your browser

Go Grey checks the address of the tab you're using to match it with your chosen sites. It saves your domain list, grayscale preferences, and pause deadline in local extension storage. A tab-specific choice uses session storage and is discarded when that tab closes. Go Grey does not record your browsing history or read the contents of posts, photos, or videos. The browser applies the grayscale effect when it draws the page.

## Your controls

Open **Manage sites** to remove an added domain and its saved preference. You can switch an included site off in the popup. Uninstalling Go Grey clears its local settings.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be raised through [Go Grey support](https://github.com/rami-maalouf/go-grey/issues). The extension's use of information is limited to providing the grayscale controls described here and complies with the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies#limited-use), including its Limited Use requirements.
